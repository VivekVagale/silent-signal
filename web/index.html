MORSE REFERENCE
Timing is learned from you: the dot/dash boundary and letter/word gaps adapt to how fast you signal (1-D k-means on a log scale). Blinks and finger presses use Google MediaPipe landmark models; flashes use plain image brightness. Everything runs in your browser. Hand gestures are MediaPipe's 7 built-in shapes used as word shortcuts, not full sign language.
